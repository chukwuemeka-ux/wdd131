Website Planning Document

Prince Motherland Solar & Electrical Solutions

1. Site Name

Prince Motherland Solar & Electrical Solutions

I chose this name because it clearly identifies the brand and communicates its focus on reliable solar power and electrical services for homes and businesses.

2. Site Purpose

The website will introduce electrical wiring, solar PV installation and maintenance, CCTV and security installations, and preventive maintenance services. Visitors will be able to learn about the services, browse example projects, and send a service inquiry using a contact form.

3. Audience Scenarios

  • What solar installation options are available for my home or business?
  • How can I request a quotation for electrical wiring or CCTV installation?
  • What maintenance services are available for an existing solar power system?

4. Color Scheme

Deep navy — #102A43
Header, headings, and footer.

Solar gold — #F4B400
Highlights, call-to-action buttons, and decorative accents.

Warm white — #F8FAFC
Page background and content areas.

5. Typography

Montserrat — Page title, section headings, and navigation.

Open Sans — Paragraphs, lists, labels, and form text.
